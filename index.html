I think of businesses the way I once thought of aircraft and weapons systems. They fail in patterns, and the patterns are foreseeable.

I spent twenty years flying for the Air Force, then led teams that modeled systems risk for the Department of Defense alongside the largest defense primes. After the service I built and sold a defense contracting firm, most of it while living on a sailboat in the Caribbean and raising my daughter across thirteen countries.

Same engineering discipline, different arena. These days I help founders and acquirers see where the structure won’t hold, and shore it up before the weight arrives.
